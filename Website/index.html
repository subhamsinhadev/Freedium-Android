Read Medium. Without the Walls.

Freedium is the sleek, open-source Android application that seamlessly bypasses Medium’s paywall, allowing you to read all member-only articles without restrictions.

Download APK (v1.2.0) Go to Source (GitHub)

Latest Version: v1.2.0 | Released: July 29, 2025

Why Use Freedium?

A fast, seamless, and beautiful reading experience.

Bypass Paywall

Access all "Member-only" Medium articles without needing an account, login, or subscription.

Clipboard Auto-detect

The app automatically detects copied Medium links, allowing you to open articles instantly without copy-pasting.

Material You Design

A smooth, responsive interface following the modern Material Design 3 (MD3) and Dynamic Theming principles.

Seamless Sharing

Simply use the 'Share via Link' option from the Medium app and select Freedium. Boom—it opens instantly.

Optimized & Fast

Built for speed using native Java, providing a fast, clean reading experience with minimal distractions.

Fully Open Source

Freedium is a **FOSS** (Free and Open Source Software) project, licensed under MIT. Contributions are welcome!
